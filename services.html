What We Do

Our Core Services

Comprehensive legal support, counselling, and advocacy rooted in faith and driven by justice.

Home › Services

How We Serve

Each program is designed to meet the real needs of individuals and families across Pakistan.

01
⚖

Legal Aid Program

Our flagship program provides free, professional legal assistance to individuals and families who cannot afford private representation.

  • Initial legal consultation and case assessment
  • Case evaluation and legal information
  • Referrals to specialized legal resources
  • Full legal representation in courts
  • Follow-up support throughout proceedings
02
♦

Human Rights & Justice

We stand as advocates for fundamental human rights, defending the dignity and freedoms of every individual.

Our work includes responding to incidents of injustice and persecution, such as the Jaranwala incident, where we provided legal counsel and support to affected communities, ensuring their voices are heard and their rights protected under the law.

03
★

Counselling & Social Support

Beyond legal matters, we provide compassionate counselling and social support to help individuals navigate life’s challenges.

  • Family conflict resolution and mediation
  • Social dispute counselling
  • Emotional and social pressures support
  • Family welfare guidance and resources
04
♥

Legal Rights Awareness

We bridge the knowledge gap between citizens and the law, empowering communities with the information they need to protect themselves.

Through community meetings, church networks, and educational workshops, we ensure that every person understands their fundamental legal rights and how to exercise them effectively.

05
⚗

Nationwide Community Outreach

Our legal awareness network extends across Pakistan, leveraging partnerships with churches and community leaders to reach those in need.

We work alongside local congregations and community organizations to deliver legal education, resources, and direct assistance to underserved regions throughout the country.

06
✿

Awareness Initiatives

We organize and participate in various programs designed to educate the public about legal rights and available resources.

Our initiatives include seminars, community meetings, legal education programs, public awareness campaigns, and referral services to connect individuals with the support they need.

Who We Serve

Dedicated to Those in Need

Grace Fellowship Law Firm extends its services to all underserved and marginalized members of society, with a special focus on the most vulnerable.

  • Low-income families and individuals
  • Women facing domestic violence or legal disputes
  • Minor children in need of legal protection
  • Religious minorities facing discrimination
  • Elderly citizens with documentation needs
  • Workers seeking justice for exploitation
