FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our legal services.

Is legal aid really free?

Yes. Our legal aid services are completely free for individuals and families who cannot afford private representation. We also provide professional legal services at accessible rates for those who can afford them — every paid engagement helps fund free cases.

How do I know if I qualify for free legal aid?

Our team will assess your case during the initial consultation. We consider your financial situation, the nature of your case, and your eligibility under our legal aid criteria. Contact us for a free assessment.

What types of cases do you handle?

We handle family disputes, domestic violence cases, property matters, criminal allegations, human rights violations, discrimination cases, and community legal issues. Our team also provides counselling and rights awareness programs.

How long does it take to get a response?

We respond to all inquiries within 24 hours. Emergency situations receive immediate attention. For urgent matters, WhatsApp or call us directly.

Is my information kept confidential?

Absolutely. All communications and case details are kept strictly confidential. Your privacy and dignity are our top priorities. We follow professional legal ethics and data protection standards.

Can I visit your office in person?

Yes. Walk-in consultations are available during office hours (Mon-Sat, 9AM-6PM). We recommend calling ahead to ensure a team member is available to meet with you.
