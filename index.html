Institutional Profile

Executive Overview

Grace Fellowship Law Firm is a community-focused legal aid initiative of Grace Bible Fellowship Church Pakistan, established to provide legal assistance, counselling and rights awareness to individuals and families who face injustice and cannot afford professional legal services.

The initiative began its community service activities in 2022 and was formally registered in June 2023.

"The organization is particularly committed to reaching people who are economically vulnerable and may otherwise be unable to access appropriate legal assistance."

Core Mission

What Drives Us

Our mission is to provide accessible legal assistance and social support to vulnerable individuals and families while promoting legal awareness throughout Pakistan.

  • Legal Aid
  • Human Rights Support
  • Counselling
  • Legal & Social Rights Awareness
  • Community Outreach

Our Services

Core Programs

Professional legal assistance, counselling and rights awareness.

01

Legal Aid Program

The core service. Initial consultation, case assessment, legal information, referral to advocates and representation in qualifying matters.

02

Human Rights & Justice

We promote respect for human dignity and fundamental rights. Our advocacy addresses issues affecting vulnerable individuals and communities.

03

Counselling & Social Support

Legal problems have social dimensions. Counselling assists individuals facing family conflicts, social disputes and personal pressures.

04

Legal Rights Awareness

Many suffer injustice because they do not know their rights. Our programs address this knowledge gap through community meetings.

05

Nationwide Community Outreach

We develop a nationwide legal awareness network with churches, community leaders and social organizations.

06

Awareness Initiatives

Seminars, community meetings, rights education, human rights campaigns, counselling sessions and referral networks.

Who We Serve

Grace Fellowship Law Firm primarily seeks to assist those who need it most — people facing injustice without the financial means to access professional legal services.

  • Low-income families
  • Individuals unable to afford legal representation
  • Victims of injustice
  • Vulnerable families requiring guidance
  • People facing social conflicts
  • Communities with limited legal access
