Who We Are

Grace Fellowship Law Firm

A community-focused legal aid initiative of Grace Bible Fellowship Church, established to support individuals and families who face injustice but cannot afford the cost of professional legal representation.

The work began in 2022 and the organization was formally registered in June 2023. For more than three years, the Law Firm has been serving deserving individuals and families across Pakistan through legal assistance, counselling, rights awareness and community support.

"No deserving person should be denied access to justice because they cannot afford a lawyer."
🎯

Our Mission

To provide accessible legal and social support to vulnerable individuals, families and communities — regardless of their ability to pay.

🌅

What Drives Us

We Work To…

Every activity of the Law Firm flows from one conviction: justice must never become inaccessible simply because someone is poor.

  • Provide free or subsidized legal assistance to deserving people
  • Support victims of injustice and human rights violations
  • Help families understand their legal rights and available remedies
  • Provide counselling for social and family-related problems
  • Conduct legal and social rights awareness programs
  • Connect vulnerable people with qualified legal professionals
  • Advocate for dignity, equality and justice
  • Strengthen communities through education and awareness

Our Services

What We Do

Five pillars of service, delivered with professionalism and compassion.

01
🛡️

Free & Affordable Legal Aid

We assist deserving individuals who cannot afford professional legal services. Depending on circumstances and resources, support may include:

  • Initial legal consultation & case assessment
  • Legal guidance & understanding procedures
  • Referral to appropriate advocates
  • Legal representation for qualifying cases
02
⚖️

Human Rights & Justice

We support people facing injustice and promote respect for fundamental rights — particularly individuals and families disadvantaged by poverty, social vulnerability or lack of awareness.

03
💬

Counselling Services

Many legal problems are connected with deeper social and family challenges. We provide guidance for:

  • Family disputes & domestic conflicts
  • Child and family welfare concerns
  • Community disputes & social pressure
  • Referral to appropriate professionals where needed
04
📢

Legal Rights Awareness

Prevention through education. Our awareness activities help communities understand basic legal and human rights, constitutional protections, legal procedures and how to seek lawful assistance.

05
🤝

Community Outreach

We work beyond the courtroom — through community meetings, awareness campaigns, church networks and outreach programs — reaching people who otherwise have no access to legal information.

06
📋

Areas of Work

Our activities may include matters involving human rights, family and civil matters, criminal matters, labour concerns, property disputes, legal documentation and the rights of vulnerable people — subject to professional assessment.

Serving Those Who Cannot Afford Justice

For a low-income family, even an initial consultation can become a barrier. Grace Fellowship Law Firm seeks to bridge that gap. Cases are assessed individually and assistance is provided according to circumstances, available resources and professional legal considerations.

  • Have genuine legal needs
  • Face injustice or exploitation
  • Cannot afford professional legal fees
  • Need guidance on their legal options
  • Require help navigating the justice system
