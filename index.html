Who We Are

A Ministry of Justice & Compassion

Grace Fellowship Law Firm was established with a singular vision: to provide exceptional legal counsel guided by unwavering principles of justice, faith, and service to our community.

The initiative began its community service activities in 2022 and was formally registered in June 2023.

Our Leadership

Peter Charles Sahotra

Chairman — Grace Fellowship Law Firm

Peter Charles Sahotra leads Grace Fellowship Law Firm with unwavering commitment to justice and tireless efforts in serving those who cannot afford legal representation.

Under his guidance, the firm has grown into a nationwide network providing free legal aid, counselling, and rights awareness to thousands of families across Pakistan.

We are committed to ensuring that every person, regardless of their financial situation, has access to justice and legal assistance.
