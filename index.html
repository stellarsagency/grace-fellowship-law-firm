Executive Overview

A Ministry of Justice & Compassion

Grace Fellowship Law Firm was born out of a deep commitment to serve the marginalized and vulnerable members of our communities through accessible, professional, and free legal assistance.

The initiative began in 2022 as a vision within Grace Bible Fellowship Church Pakistan to address the growing need for legal support among those who could not afford it. By June 2023, the firm was officially registered and began formal operations, combining legal expertise with a Christ-centered approach to justice and advocacy.

“Justice is not a privilege for the few, but a right for all. We are called to be advocates for those who cannot speak for themselves.”

⚖

Institutional Identity

A registered legal aid firm operating as a ministry project of Grace Bible Fellowship Church Pakistan, combining professional legal practice with faith-based community service.

Our Purpose

To provide free, high-quality legal representation, counselling, and advocacy for underprivileged individuals and families across Pakistan, guided by Christian values of love, justice, and dignity.

Our Mission

What We Stand For

Our mission is to uphold justice and provide legal assistance to the underserved, reflecting God’s love through professional service and community engagement.

  • ✓ Providing free legal aid and representation to those unable to afford legal services
  • ✓ Offering legal counselling and guidance to individuals facing complex legal challenges
  • ✓ Conducting rights awareness programs to educate communities about their legal rights
  • ✓ Advocating for systemic change and social justice at local and national levels
  • ✓ Building capacity within communities to understand and navigate the legal system

Who We Serve

Dedicated to Those in Need

Grace Fellowship Law Firm extends its services to all underserved and marginalized members of society, with a special focus on the most vulnerable.

  • ✓ Low-income families and individuals who cannot afford legal representation
  • ✓ Women facing domestic violence, inheritance disputes, or family legal issues
  • ✓ Minor children in need of legal protection, custody arrangements, or guardianship
  • ✓ Religious minorities facing discrimination or seeking legal protection of their rights
  • ✓ Elderly citizens requiring assistance with legal documentation and property matters
  • ✓ Workers and laborers seeking justice for exploitation, unpaid wages, or workplace abuse
