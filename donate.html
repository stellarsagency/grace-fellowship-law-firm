Why Donate

Your Support Changes Lives

Many families in Pakistan face injustice but cannot afford legal representation. Your donation helps bridge this gap.

Grace Fellowship Law Firm provides free and subsidized legal aid to individuals and families who face injustice and cannot afford professional legal services. Every donation, no matter the size, helps us reach more people in need.

“When a vulnerable family cannot afford a lawyer, the absence of financial resources should not prevent that family from seeking lawful assistance.”
💰

Where Your Money Goes

Legal Aid: Court fees, advocate coordination, case documentation
Counselling: Family and individual counselling sessions
Awareness: Community rights education programs
Outreach: Nationwide church network coordination

📈

Our Impact

Since 2022, we have assisted numerous families with legal aid, counselling and rights awareness. Your support helps us continue and expand this critical work across Pakistan.
