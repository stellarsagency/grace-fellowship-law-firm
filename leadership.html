Chairman

Peter Charles Sahotra

Chairman — Grace Fellowship Law Firm

Peter Charles Sahotra leads Grace Fellowship Law Firm with unwavering commitment to justice and tireless efforts in serving those who cannot afford legal representation. His leadership has been instrumental in establishing the firm as a trusted legal aid provider across Pakistan.

Under his guidance, the firm has grown from a small initiative into a nationwide network providing free legal aid, counselling, and rights awareness to thousands of families in need.

His vision is simple yet powerful: to ensure that no person in Pakistan is denied access to justice simply because they cannot afford a lawyer.

"Justice should never be a privilege for the wealthy. Every person, regardless of their financial situation, deserves the right to seek legal help and defend their dignity."

Our Foundation

Rooted in Faith, Growing in Service

Grace Fellowship Law Firm operates under the spiritual guidance and support of Grace Bible Fellowship Church Pakistan. Our roots in faith drive our commitment to justice, compassion, and service to the community.

We believe that true legal advocacy is not just about winning cases — it is about restoring dignity, protecting rights, and serving God by serving others.
