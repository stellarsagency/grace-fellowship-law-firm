Patron's Message

Message from the Patron-in-Chief

"Justice is not a privilege; it is a right."

It is my great privilege to welcome you to Grace Fellowship Law Firm, a ministry-driven legal initiative committed to standing with victims of injustice and providing hope, guidance, and access to justice — particularly for those who are unable to afford professional legal assistance.

Our work is founded on a simple but powerful conviction: every human being deserves dignity, protection, equality before the law, and an opportunity to be heard. Legal difficulties can overwhelm individuals and families, especially when they lack financial resources, knowledge of their rights, or access to trustworthy legal counsel. We seek to bridge that gap.

Grace Fellowship Law Firm was established with the vision of combining legal assistance, counselling, awareness, advocacy, and community support. Since beginning our work, we have sought to assist deserving families and individuals facing injustice, discrimination, exploitation, family disputes, property matters, criminal allegations, and other legal challenges.

Our commitment is not limited to the courtroom. We believe that prevention is equally important. Through legal awareness and education, we want people to understand their constitutional and legal rights and to know where and how they can seek lawful remedies when those rights are violated.

As a Christian initiative, our approach is guided by the principles of truth, compassion, integrity, mercy, and justice. We do not believe that justice should depend upon a person's financial position, social status, religion, or influence. We aspire to serve with impartiality and professionalism while respecting the rule of law and the dignity of every person.

I am deeply grateful to our legal professionals, volunteers, supporters, community leaders, and partners who have contributed their time, expertise, and resources to this mission. I especially appreciate the leadership of Chairman Peter Charles Sahotra and Director Advocate Shahbaz Fazal, together with the entire legal and support team, for their commitment to serving vulnerable communities.

Looking ahead, our vision is to develop Grace Fellowship Law Firm into a stronger platform for accessible legal assistance, legal literacy, victim support, and social justice advocacy. We want to empower people not merely to seek justice after they have suffered harm, but also to understand their rights and take informed, lawful steps to protect themselves and their families.

I invite lawyers, professionals, churches, civil society organisations, philanthropists, and people of goodwill to join us in this important mission.

Let us work together to build a society where justice is accessible, dignity is respected, rights are protected, and no deserving person is left alone simply because they cannot afford a lawyer.

May God give us wisdom, courage, integrity, and compassion as we continue this journey of service.

Pastor Tariq Rehmat

Patron-in-Chief, Grace Fellowship Law Firm

Chairman

Peter Charles Sahotra

Chairman — Grace Fellowship Law Firm

Peter Charles Sahotra leads Grace Fellowship Law Firm with unwavering commitment to justice and tireless efforts in serving those who cannot afford legal representation.

His leadership has been instrumental in establishing the firm as a trusted legal aid provider across Pakistan. Under his guidance, the firm has grown from a small initiative into a nationwide network providing free legal aid, counselling, and rights awareness to thousands of families in need.

His vision is simple yet powerful: to ensure that no person in Pakistan is denied access to justice simply because they cannot afford a lawyer.

Peter Charles Sahotra believes that every individual deserves the opportunity to seek justice, and he has dedicated his life to making that belief a reality for the most vulnerable members of society.

"Justice should never be a privilege for the wealthy. Every person, regardless of their financial situation, deserves the right to seek legal help and defend their dignity."

Advocate Shahbaz Fazal Saroya

Director's Message

Message from the Director of Legal Services

At Grace Fellowship Law Firm, we believe that access to justice should not depend on a person's financial circumstances. Our mission is to provide professional legal guidance, support victims of injustice, and help individuals and families understand and exercise their legal rights.

Through our legal services, counselling, awareness initiatives, and advocacy, we strive to serve with integrity, professionalism, compassion, and respect for the rule of law. We are particularly committed to supporting vulnerable and deserving people who may otherwise struggle to obtain appropriate legal assistance.

Our goal is not only to respond to legal problems but also to promote legal awareness, prevention, dignity, and equal protection under the law.

We remain committed to standing with those seeking justice and to using our legal knowledge and experience responsibly in the service of people and society.

Advocate Shahbaz Fazal Saroya

Director of Legal Services, Grace Fellowship Law Firm
